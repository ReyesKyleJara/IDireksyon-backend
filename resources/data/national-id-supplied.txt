NATIONAL ID (PhilSys)
LINK: [https://philsys.gov.ph/the-national-id/](https://philsys.gov.ph/the-national-id/) 
FOR FILIPINO RESIDENTS AND FOR FOREIGNER RESIDENTS IN THE PHILIPPINES:
ID NAME: Philippine Identification System (PhilSys National ID)
ISSUING AGENCY: Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
PURPOSE: Serves as a single, foundational government-issued proof of identity to simplify public and private transactions, promote financial inclusion, and streamline access to social protection services.
DESCRIPTION: An official government-issued identification system containing a unique permanent PhilSys Number (PSN), biometric data (facial image, iris scan, fingerprints), and a QR code for secure digital verification. Available in physical card, paper format (ePhilID), and mobile app (Digital National ID) versions.
ELIGIBILITY: All Filipino citizens (living in the Philippines or abroad) and foreign residents residing in the Philippines for at least six (6) months.
VALIDITY PERIOD:
- Filipino Citizens: Permanent / Lifetime validity.
- Foreign Residents: Valid for one (1) year, renewable annually upon confirmation of legal status.
REQUIREMENTS NEW APPLICATION (FILIPINO CITIZENS):
1. PRIMARY SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS The registrant is encouraged to bring any ONE (1) of the following primary documents (Original Copy):
- PSA-issued Certificate of Live Birth AND one (1) government-issued ID bearing full name, front-facing photo, and signature/thumbmark
- Philippine Passport or ePassport (DFA)
- Unified Multi-purpose Identification (UMID) Card (GSIS or SSS)
- Student Permit, Non-Professional Driver’s License, or Professional Driver’s License (LTO) (Note: In case of discrepancies between the PSA Birth Certificate and the government ID presented, the entry in the PSA Birth Certificate shall prevail).
2. SECONDARY SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS If no primary document is available, the applicant may submit any ONE (1) of the following secondary documents (Original Copy):
- PSA-issued / NSO-issued Certificate of Live Birth with Birth Reference Number (BReN)
- Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO)-issued Certificate of Live Birth
- PSA-issued Report of Birth or Certificate of Foundling
- Integrated Bar of the Philippines (IBP) ID
- Professional Regulatory Commission (PRC) ID
- Seaman’s Book (Seafarer’s Record Book) or Seafarer’s Identity Document (Seaman’s ID)
- Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA) ID
- Senior Citizen’s ID (OSCA / LGU)
- Social Security System (SSS) ID
- Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) ID
- License to Own or Possess Firearms (LTOPF)
- National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) Clearance
- Police Clearance / Police ID
- Solo Parent’s ID
- Person with Disability (PWD) ID (NCDA, Mayor's Office, DSWD, or DOH-partnered organizations)
- Voter’s ID (COMELEC)
- Postal ID (PHLPost)
- Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) Card
- PhilHealth ID
- Special Resident Retiree’s Visa (SRRV)
- National ID or Residence ID from other countries
- Professional Identification Card (MARINA)
- Civil Service Commission (CSC) Eligibility Card
- Dependent’s ID (AFP / PNP)
- Retiree’s ID (PNP, AFP, PCG)
- Conductor’s License (LTO)
- Philippine Veterans Affairs Office (PVAO) Pensioner’s ID
- Tribal Certificate/ID (Tribal Affairs Office) or Certificate of Confirmation/Membership (NCIP / ICC / IP / NCMF)
3. ADDITIONAL SECONDARY DOCUMENTS (Requires photo, signature/thumbmark, full name, permanent address, and birthdate):
- Employee ID
- School ID (for pre-school, elementary, and high school; signature/thumbmark of ID owner is not required)
- City / Municipal ID
- Barangay Clearance / Barangay Certificate / Barangay ID
- Voter’s Certification (COMELEC)
- Prison Record or Certificate of Detention
PREREQUISITES / DEPENDENCIES:
- PSA Birth Certificate or any accepted primary/secondary supporting document.
APPLICATION FEE / COSTS:
- First-Time Registration: Free of Charge (₱0.00).
- ePhilID Paper Copy & Digital ID: Free of Charge (₱0.00).
APPLICATION PROCESS / STEPS (NEW APPLICATION - WALK-IN):
1. Visit Registration Center: Proceed to any operational PhilSys Registration Center (Fixed PSA offices, municipal halls, or mall-based booths). No online pre-registration or appointment is needed.
2. Document Screening: Present your original primary or secondary supporting documents to the Registration Kit Operator.
3. Biometric Capture: Have your demographic data validated and biometrics captured (front-facing photograph, 10-fingerprint scan, dual-iris scan).
4. Review Information: Carefully review and confirm your encoded demographic details on the display screen.
5. Collect Transaction Slip: Receive your official transaction slip containing your unique Transaction Reference Number (TRN).
DIGITAL NATIONAL ID GENERATION (ONLINE STEP-BY-STEP):
1. Download the eGovPH Mobile App or visit [https://philsys.gov.ph/](https://philsys.gov.ph/) 
2. Create an account using your active mobile number and select Digital National ID.
3. Enter your Transaction Reference Number (TRN) from your registration slip.
4. Complete the mandatory live facial recognition check on your smartphone.
5. Access, view, or screenshot your official Digital National ID for immediate use in transactions.
RENEWAL PROCESS: Not applicable for adult Filipino citizens (lifetime validity). Mandatory biometric recapture and renewal are required for foreign residents (annually) and minors upon reaching age 5 and age 18.
REPLACEMENT PROCESS (UPDATE DETAILS / LOST CARD):
- Updating Demographic Data: Visit a registration center with supporting documents (e.g., Marriage Certificate or corrected Birth Certificate) for validation.
- Lost / Stolen Card: Present a notarized Affidavit of Loss (or police blotter for stolen cards), a primary/secondary ID, pay a replacement fee (₱200–₱400), and undergo biometric verification.
- Damaged Card: Surrender physical damaged card at a registration center alongside valid supporting ID and undergo biometric verification (₱135–₱400; free if due to manufacturing defect).
PROCESSING TIME:
- Digital National ID: Instant generation online via eGovPH app after registration.
- ePhilID (Paper Format): Printed on the spot or available for pickup at registration centers.
- Physical Polycarbonate Card: Produced by BSP and delivered via PHLPost to the registered home address.
GOVERNMENT OFFICES / LOCATIONS FOR SANTA MARIA, BULACAN RESIDENTS:
- Fixed Provincial Office: PSA Bulacan Provincial Office (Malolos, Bulacan).
- Local Municipal Centers: Santa Maria Municipal Hall / LGU PhilSys Booths.
- Mall-Based Booths: SM City Marilao, SM City Baliwag, Robinsons Place Malolos.
OFFICE HOURS: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (excluding public holidays; mall sites follow mall operating hours).
