PASSPORT
LINK: [https://passport.gov.ph/](https://passport.gov.ph/)
FOR FILIPINO RESIDENTS AND FOR FOREIGNER RESIDENTS IN THE PHILIPPINES:
ID NAME: Philippine Passport (ePassport)
ISSUING AGENCY: Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA)
PURPOSE: An official identification card and travel document used to certify the identity and Philippine citizenship of the holder for international travel, identity verification, and general transaction purposes across public and private sectors.
DESCRIPTION: An official government-issued travel document and primary ID for Filipino citizens. It contains your photo, personal details, and secure digital data used for identity verification and international travel.
ELIGIBILITY: All Filipino citizens (by birth or naturalization) residing in the Philippines or abroad with valid legal status and a clean record with court/travel restriction clearance.
VALIDITY PERIOD: The passport is valid for ten (10) years for adult applicants aged 18 and above, and five (5) years for minor applicants below 18 years old.
REQUIREMENTS FOR NEW APPLICATION (ADULT APPLICANTS - 18 YRS OLD AND ABOVE):
1. Confirmed DFA Online Appointment booking printout and completed Passport Application Form.
2. Proof of Identity (PRIMARY IDs) Submit any one (1) of the acceptable primary IDs listed below. Bring the Original copy and one (1) clear Photocopy:
- PhilSys National ID / ePhilID
- Social Security System (SSS) Card
- Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) Card
- Unified Multi-Purpose ID (UMID)
- Driver’s License (LTO)
- Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) ID
- Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA) ID / iDOLE
- Commission on Elections (COMELEC) Voter's ID or Voter's Certification (issued at Head Office, Intramuros)
- Philippine Postal ID (issued November 2016 onwards)
- Senior Citizen ID / PWD ID
- Airman License / Seaman’s Book / PNP License
3. Proof of Citizenship (MANDATORY DOCUMENT) Bring the Original copy and one (1) clear Photocopy of the following:
- Original PSA-issued Birth Certificate on Security Paper (SecPa)
- Local Civil Registrar (LCR) copy of Birth Certificate (required if the PSA Birth Certificate is unreadable or lacks details)
- Form 137 / Transcript of Records, Voter's Registration Record, or Baptismal Certificate (required to support late-registered Birth Certificates)
4. Additional Requirement for Married Women Only if the applicant opts to adopt her husband's surname:
- Original PSA Marriage Certificate on Security Paper (SecPa) and one (1) clear Photocopy
REQUIREMENTS FOR MINOR APPLICANTS (17 YRS OLD BELOW):
- Personal appearance of minor applicant and mother or authorized adult companion.
- Confirmed online appointment booking printout (unless eligible for courtesy lane).
- Original PSA Birth Certificate of the minor on Security Paper.
- Valid Primary ID of parent/legal guardian (Original and photocopy).
- Marriage Certificate of parents (if legitimate) or Special Power of Attorney (SPA) / Affidavit of Consent if traveling or applying without parents.
PREREQUISITES / DEPENDENCIES:
- PSA Birth Certificate (issued by Civil Registrar / PSA)
- At least one (1) valid Primary Government ID
APPLICATION FEE / COSTS:
- Regular Processing: ₱950.00
- Expedited Processing: ₱1,200.00
- Convenience Fee: ₱50.00 (charged per applicant by DFA online payment channels)
- Optional Courier Delivery Fee: ~₱150.00 (if door-to-door delivery option is selected during booking)
APPLICATION PROCESS / STEPS (NEW APPLICATION):
1. Online Booking: Access the official DFA portal [passport.gov.ph](http://passport.gov.ph), select your preferred consular office, and pick an available date and time slot.
2. Data Encoding: Select "NEW" under Application Type and complete the online application form with accurate personal details.
3. Payment: Pay the processing fee online using a credit/debit card, e-wallet, or authorized payment partner within the designated time limit.
4. Packet Printing: Download and print the appointment confirmation email, barcode, e-receipt, and completed application form on A4 size paper.
5. Personal Appearance: Arrive at the selected DFA Consular Office 15–30 minutes before your scheduled slot in proper smart casual attire.
6. Verification & Biometrics: Undergo document screening, data encoding verification, and biometric capture (photograph, fingerprints, digital signature).
7. Issuance / Delivery: Choose between picking up the passport at the consular office or having it delivered via courier.
RENEWAL PROCESS (ADULT RENEWAL STEP-BY-STEP):
1. Book Appointment: Visit [passport.gov.ph](http://passport.gov.ph), select "RENEWAL" under Application Type, and select your preferred consular office, date, and time.
2. Complete Data & Pay: Fill out personal information, select Regular or Expedited processing, and pay the fee online via e-wallet, credit/debit card, or payment center.
3. Print Packet: Print the confirmation email PDF packet (Application Form, Confirmation Slip, and Receipt) on A4 paper.
4. Prepare Physical Documents:
   - Printed application form and payment receipt.
   - Current/Expired ePassport (Original).
   - Photocopy of Passport Data Page (front page containing details).
   - Valid Primary Government ID (Original and 1 photocopy).
   - If changing surname due to marriage: Original PSA Marriage Certificate + photocopy.
5. Personal Appearance & Biometrics: Arrive 15–30 minutes before your schedule at the DFA site, present documents at verification, and proceed to biometric capture (photo, fingerprints, signature).
6. Claim or Delivery: Receive your stamped claim stub for pick-up or set up door-to-door courier delivery.
REPLACEMENT PROCESS (LOST / DAMAGED PASSPORT):
- Lost Valid ePassport: Submit an Affidavit of Loss, Police Report, PSA Birth Certificate, valid ID, pay the penalty fee of ₱350.00, and undergo a 15-business-day clearing period before processing.
- Lost Expired ePassport: Submit an Affidavit of Loss, PSA Birth Certificate, and valid ID (treated as a new application without the 15-day clearing period).
- Damaged / Mutilated Passport: Submit an Affidavit of Mutilation, physical damaged passport, valid ID, and pay the penalty fee of ₱350.00.
PROCESSING TIME:
- Regular Processing: 10 to 12 working days
- Expedited Processing: 6 to 7 working days
PASSPORT RELEASING & CERTIFICATION:
- Passport Releasing: Present your claim stub, original current/old passport (for cancellation), and official receipt. If claimed by the representative: Authorization letter / Special Power of Attorney (SPA) and representative's valid ID.
- Passport Certification: Certificate of Passport Issuance or Certification of Appearance requested directly at DFA Consular Offices for legal, employment, or travel verification purposes.
